VeriNest for macOS
==================

1. Drag VeriNest into the Applications folder.

2. VeriNest is free, open-source software and is not signed with a paid Apple
   developer certificate, so macOS blocks it the first time. To allow it, do ONE of:

   a) Open Terminal and run:
          xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VeriNest.app
      then open VeriNest normally.

   b) Double-click VeriNest, close the warning, then open
      System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".

   You only need to do this once.

3. Check that everything works: open the "?" (Help) menu in VeriNest and click
   "Run self-test". It should end with ALL EXAMPLES PASSED.

Your projects are stored in ~/VeriNest/projects.
Source code, help and bug reports: https://github.com/mortezapourhoseini/verinest
